Tesla · Production issue response

Unblocked a production-critical half-shaft installation issue

Cybertruck vehicles were making abnormal noise while moving. A teardown showed the half-shaft splines wearing into each other.

The root cause was a C-clip that wasn't fully seated during installation. If the clip's gap wasn't oriented correctly, there was a 20–30% chance it wouldn't catch.

I ran the response on two tracks. On the line, I added an operator check to rotate the clip to the correct orientation before install, validated it on 10 vehicles in an end-of-shift trial, then rolled it out across the line.

In parallel, an engineer and the supplier worked on a permanent design change. I worked on and off hours and kept executives updated on mitigation status.

  • Root-cause analysis
  • Containment
  • Line validation
  • Supplier + engineering
  • Exec communication

Zoox · Process design

Created the Engineering Test Asset Committee (ETAC)

Requests for test assets, such as lighting rigs, sensor-contamination bots, and vehicle-level builds, arrived ad hoc from sister teams. Asks added mid-build caused scope creep and put timelines for downstream work at risk.

I created ETAC to centralize the requests. Every asset now starts with one Jira intake and a one-pager covering the capability, timeline, and milestone.

A standing forum brings prototyping, controller validation, EMC, optics, supply chain, and HW/SW integration to one table. The committee challenges whether each requirement is needed, looks for existing assets to reuse, and splits big builds into phases. That is how knowledge moves between teams.

Each build is scheduled and tracked with Gantt charts, weekly updates, and epics, for current and new assets alike.

  • Process design
  • Requirements
  • Cross-functional forum
  • Jira + Smartsheet
  • Phased delivery
